Grace Lyell

Grace Lyell is an artist of British origin raised between France, Holland and Italy.

She's built a singular career at the intersection of dance, direction, choreography and visual creation. After eight years performing with internationally acclaimed dance companies, she now works as a freelance film director, choreographer and photographer in Paris — collaborating across fashion, film, music and the arts.
